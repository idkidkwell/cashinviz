# Code of Conduct

This project follows a short, plain version of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).

## What's expected

- Be kind. Assume the other person is acting in good faith.
- Disagree on the technical merits, never on the person.
- Welcome newcomers. Everyone was new once.
- Privacy and security are first-class — treat people's threat
  models as their threat models, not yours.

## What gets you removed

- Harassment of any kind, in or out of the project's spaces.
- Doxing or threatening to dox anyone.
- Posting other people's private information without consent
  (addresses, real names, employers, wallet links to identities).
- Sustained disruption (spam, off-topic crusading, brigading).
- Any conduct that, in the maintainers' reasonable judgement, is
  inappropriate for a professional setting.

## Reporting

Email **conduct@cashinviz.com** (placeholder until launch — until
then, open a private issue or contact a maintainer directly).

Reports are kept confidential. Don't retaliate against someone for
reporting in good faith — that itself is grounds for removal.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will:

1. Investigate the report and talk to both sides.
2. Decide on a proportionate response — anything from a private
   note to a permanent ban.
3. Communicate the decision to the reporter (and the subject if
   appropriate).

Decisions are final but may be appealed in writing within 30 days.
